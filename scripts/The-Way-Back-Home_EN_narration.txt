The Way Back Home. A true story from the Bible.

<break time="3.0s" />

In the beginning, God made everything. He made the sky, the trees, the flowers, and the birds. Then God made a man named Adam and a woman named Eve. God loved them, and they loved being with Him. Their garden home, Eden, was perfect.

<break time="3.0s" />

God gave Adam and Eve every tree in the garden to enjoy. Only one tree was not for them. “Do not eat from that tree,” God said. “If you do, you will die.” It was one simple rule from a Father who loved them.

<break time="3.0s" />

One day, a sneaky serpent spoke to Eve. “Did God really say that?” he asked. “That fruit won’t hurt you. It will make you like God!” But the serpent was lying. He wanted Eve to stop trusting God.

<break time="3.0s" />

Eve looked at the fruit. It looked so good. She took a bite. Then she gave some to Adam, and he ate it too. Uh-oh. For the very first time, people had disobeyed God. The Bible calls this sin.

<break time="3.0s" />

Sin changed everything. Adam and Eve felt afraid and ashamed. God covered them with clothes made from animal skins. But that meant innocent animals had to die. Adam and Eve could no longer live in their garden home. Yet God already had a plan to bring His people home.

<break time="3.0s" />

Adam and Eve had children, and their children had children, all the way down to you and me. Even though many years have passed, we still have the same sin problem that separates us from God. The Bible says that all of us have sinned, just like Adam and Eve.

<break time="3.0s" />

God gave us ten rules, the Ten Commandments. Have you ever told a lie? Even once? Have you ever taken something that wasn’t yours? If yes, that makes you a liar and a thief. That doesn’t feel very good, does it? And that is only two of the ten! Like Adam and Eve, you have broken God’s law, and that makes you guilty.

<break time="3.0s" />

Like a mirror, God’s law shows us our sin, but it cannot wash it away. Just like Adam and Eve were locked out of their home long ago, so are we. The Bible says that if we die with our sin, we will go to a place called hell, far away from God forever. That is very bad news.

<break time="3.0s" />

So how can we get rid of our sin? We might think we can wash it away by being extra good, saying sorry, or going to church. But no matter how hard we scrub, the marks will not come off. Being good tomorrow can’t erase the wrongs we did today. Remember the animals that died to cover Adam and Eve? We need someone to take our place too.

<break time="3.0s" />

Here is the good news! That someone is Jesus. God loved the world so much that He sent His only Son. Jesus said, “I am the door. Whoever comes in through Me will be saved.” People who know Jesus tell others, “Come and see! There is a way back to God!”

<break time="3.0s" />

Jesus never sinned, not even once. Like the innocent animals that died for Adam and Eve, Jesus died on a cross in our place. All our sin was put on Him, and He took the punishment we deserved. When we trust Him, our sin is washed away, and we are covered with His goodness.

<break time="3.0s" />

Now you have a choice to make. You can tell God, “I am sorry for my sin. I believe Jesus died in my place. Please forgive me and save me!” That means turning away from your sin and trusting Jesus. Or you can say, “No thanks,” and walk away, like some people do. Jesus loves you, and He lets you choose.

<break time="3.0s" />

Three days after Jesus died, His friends found His tomb empty. Jesus was alive again! He beat sin and death. Look! The marks are gone. Everyone who trusts Jesus is dressed in His goodness, as clean and white as snow. And now the gate is open. The way home is open!

<break time="3.0s" />

One day, everyone who trusts Jesus will be with Him forever. Heaven’s gates will be wide open. And Jesus will be right there, holding out His hands to welcome them home. You can still see the scars in His hands, the scars of His love.

<break time="3.0s" />

No more crying. No more hurting. No more sin. Just God’s big, warm love, forever and ever. Finally, we are home!

<break time="3.0s" />

And guess what? God will make everything new: a new heaven and a new earth, even better than Eden! Until that day, Jesus asks you to tell others the good news, so they can find the way back home too.

<break time="3.0s" />

Would you like to follow Jesus? It is as easy as A, B, C. Admit to God that you have sinned, and say you are sorry. Believe that Jesus is God’s Son, that He died for you, and that He rose again. Choose to trust Jesus and follow Him every day. “Dear God, I know I have sinned, and I am sorry. Thank You for sending Jesus to die for me and rise again. Please forgive me and make me clean. I want to follow You. In Jesus’ name, amen.” Talk with a parent, pastor, or grown-up you trust about what you prayed.

<break time="3.0s" />

For grown-ups. Try these questions after reading together. What was the one rule God gave Adam and Eve? Why do you think He gave it? Why couldn’t the people wash their marks away? What did Jesus do so we could come back to God? How do we know Jesus is alive? Who is someone you could tell the good news to this week?
