The Way Back Home. A true story from the Bible.

<break time="3.0s" />

In the beginning, God made everything: the sky, the trees, the flowers, and the birds. Then He made the first man and woman, Adam and Eve. God was with them in their garden home, Eden. They loved being with Him, and He loved them. Everything was perfect.

<break time="3.0s" />

God gave Adam and Eve every tree in the garden to enjoy, except one. “Do not eat from that tree,” God said. “If you do, you will die.” Adam pointed to the tree so Eve would remember. It was one simple rule from a Father who loved them.

<break time="3.0s" />

One day, a sneaky serpent slid down beside Eve. “Did God really say that?” he hissed. “You won’t die! That fruit will make you like God!” But the serpent was lying. He wanted Eve to stop trusting God.

<break time="3.0s" />

Eve looked at the fruit. It looked so good, so she took a bite. Then she gave some to Adam, and he ate it too. Uh-oh. For the very first time, people had disobeyed God. The Bible calls this sin. And the serpent grinned. His trick had worked.

<break time="3.0s" />

Sin changed everything. Now Adam and Eve felt afraid and ashamed. God made them clothes from animal skins. But to make them, innocent animals had to die. Then God sent them out of the garden, and an angel guarded the way back in. But God also made them a promise. One day, Someone would come to crush the serpent and open the way back home.

<break time="3.0s" />

Adam and Eve had children, and their children had children, all the way down to you and me. Look at all these people! Every one of them has sin marks, just like Adam and Eve. The Bible says we have all sinned, and our sin keeps us far from God.

<break time="3.0s" />

God gave us ten good rules, called the Ten Commandments. Have you ever told a lie? Even once? Have you ever taken something that wasn’t yours? If so, that makes you a liar and a thief. That doesn’t feel very good, does it? And that’s only two of the ten! Like Adam and Eve, we have all broken God’s law, and our sin leaves marks we can’t hide.

<break time="3.0s" />

God’s law is like a mirror. It shows us our sin, but it can’t wash it away. Sin locks us out of God’s home, just like Adam and Eve were locked out of the garden. God is good and fair, so He can’t just pretend our sin never happened. The Bible says that if we die with our sin, we will be punished in a place called hell, far away from God forever. That is very bad news.

<break time="3.0s" />

So how can we get rid of our sin? We might try to scrub it off by being extra good, saying sorry, or going to church. But no matter how hard we scrub, the marks won’t come off. Being good tomorrow can’t erase the wrong things we did today. Remember the animals that died to cover Adam and Eve? Someone has to take our place too. But who?

<break time="3.0s" />

Here is the good news! That someone is Jesus, God’s own Son. He was there in the garden with Adam and Eve, and He is the One God promised. God loved the world so much that He sent Jesus to bring us home. Jesus said, “I am the door. Whoever comes in through Me will be saved.” That’s why people who know Jesus love to tell others, “Come and see! There is a way back to God!”

<break time="3.0s" />

Jesus never sinned, not even once. But He died on a cross in our place, like the animals that died for Adam and Eve. All our sin was put on Him, and He took the punishment we deserved. Jesus did this because He loves us.

<break time="3.0s" />

Now everyone has a choice to make. Some people say, “No thanks,” and walk away. Others tell God, “I’m sorry for my sin. I believe Jesus died in my place. Please forgive me and save me!” That means turning away from sin and trusting Jesus. When we do, God takes our sin away and covers us with Jesus’ goodness. Jesus loves you, and He lets you choose.

<break time="3.0s" />

Three days after Jesus died, His friends found His tomb empty. Jesus was alive again! He had beaten sin and death! Everyone who trusts Him is as clean and white as snow. The gate is open. Jesus has made a way back home!

<break time="3.0s" />

One day, everyone who trusts Jesus will run through heaven’s open gates. And Jesus will be right there, with His arms open wide. The same Jesus who sent Adam and Eve out of the garden will welcome His children home. You can still see the scars in His hands, the scars of His love.

<break time="3.0s" />

And guess what? God will make everything new: a new heaven and a new earth, even better than Eden! Until that day, Jesus asks you to tell others the good news, so they can find the way back home too.

<break time="3.0s" />

Would you like to follow Jesus? Here is what it means: A, B, C. Admit to God that you have sinned, and say you are sorry. Believe that Jesus is God’s Son, that He died for you, and that He rose again. Choose to trust Jesus and follow Him every day. “Dear God, I know I have sinned, and I am sorry. Thank You for sending Jesus to die for me and rise again. Please forgive me and make me clean. I want to follow You. In Jesus’ name, amen.” Talk with a parent, pastor, or grown-up you trust about what you prayed.

What did Jesus do so we could come back to God? How do we know Jesus is alive? What does it mean to trust Jesus? Is that something you want to do?
